Start here

Three roles share the app. You are the Biller: you see the billing operation and the money owed, but not a clinician's private payout settings or their clinical notes.

The three roles

Who sees what

  • Biller (you): billing queue, claims, invoices, money owed, every client, the by-clinician numbers, Setup (claims + fees only).
  • Owner: the whole business picture plus the money-management side of Setup (retention, expenses, splits).
  • Clinician: only their own clients, sessions and payout.

Your left menu

  • Biller dashboard your month and commission
  • Billing queue the daily workspace
  • By clinician per-person numbers
  • Owed by clients chase list
  • Clients the roster
  • Plus Team (Notices, Messages, Tickets) and Setup + Worklist

Billing queue

/billing/paymentsbiller / owner

Your operational workspace: submit claims to insurers, then mark them collected as money lands.

The tabs (a claim's life)

  • To bill logged, not yet submitted. Sorted oldest first.
  • Awaiting payment submitted, waiting on the insurer.
  • Collected paid in full. Scoped one month at a time.
  • Self-pay client balances (shows only if any exist).
  • Written off / down settled by adjustment, never pays out.

Top ribbon (at a glance)

  • To bill count, Awaiting payment total, Collected this month.
  • Oldest open in days (turns amber at 15+).
  • Your cut so far and +pending on open.

Working the rows

  • Group by insurer or by clinician; search and filter by clinician.
  • Aging chips: 0-14 15-30 31-60 60+ days.
  • On To bill rows: a short + note (max 40 chars) the clinician can also see.
  • Awaiting rows carry an editable billed date, back-date it to when the claim really went out.
  • A ⚠ after referral flag warns a visit falls after the client's referral ended.

Buttons

  • Mark N billed stamps the submitted date on ticked To-bill claims.
  • Mark N collected records payment on Awaiting / Self-pay rows.
  • Generate CMS-1500 builds claim forms for ticked sessions.
  • Un-bill sends a claim back to To bill.
  • Write off/down then Settle records a partial or nil payment.
  • Undo reverses a collected or written-off claim.
Only collected money pays out. Submitting a claim or writing one off never adds to anyone's payout. Cash landing (marked collected) is the only thing that does.

Claims & invoices

the paperwork

Two documents leave the practice: the CMS-1500 goes to the insurer, the invoice goes to the client. Insured visits go on a claim, self-pay visits go on an invoice.

CMS-1500 claim

/billing/clients/[id]/cms1500/billing/clients/batch

A print-ready health-insurance claim form built from a client's insured sessions. Print or Save-as-PDF, then submit to the insurer.

How sessions become lines

  • One form per insurer. Sessions are grouped by payer.
  • Each CPT code occurrence is its own service line in box 24.
  • Box 24 holds 6 lines; more than 6 spills onto a continuation form ("form 2 of 3").
  • Self-pay visits never appear here.

Two views & where fields come from

  • Official form (the red facsimile) and Review sheet (plain, easy to scan). Only the visible one prints.
  • Patient / insured / diagnosis (box 21) come from the client record.
  • NPI, Tax ID, billing provider (boxes 25, 31-33) come from Setup. If unset, they print blank with a warning.

Getting there

  • From a client record: Generate CMS-1500 (all their insured visits), or tick rows and use CMS-1500 (N).
  • From the roster or queue: tick several and batch them. The button reads Print N claim / Save PDF, where N is the total number of forms across everyone.

Client invoice

/billing/clients/[id]/invoice

A bill sent to the client for what they personally owe. Two kinds, chosen automatically by the visit type.

Two kinds

  • Self-pay: the full fee for visits with no insurer.
  • Co-pay (type=copay): just the outstanding co-pay on an insured visit, each line prefixed "Co-pay:".
  • A sessions= list narrows it to exactly the visits you ticked; with none, it covers all that qualify.

Email to client

  • Email to client opens a preview: To, editable Subject, the attached PDF, Replies to the treating clinician, and an editable message.
  • Send to client sends it; the send is logged on the client's record under Emails sent.
  • No email on file blocks the send and tells you to add one first.
Known gap you flagged: when you tick a mix of self-pay and insured charges, the Invoice (N) button only carries the self-pay ones. A fix to bill a mixed selection on one invoice is queued.

Chasing money

what's still owed

Three lists of outstanding money: what clients owe, co-pays not yet collected, and insurance claims that are getting old.

Owed by clients

/billing/balancesbiller / owner

Everything clients still owe, oldest first, so you know who to chase.

Top cards

  • Total owed = self-pay balances + uncollected co-pays.
  • Self-pay balances and Co-pays not collected (jumps to the co-pays page).

The list

  • Per client: self-pay owed, co-pay not collected, total, and Oldest visit date.
  • Sorted oldest first, chase the top of the list.
  • Invoice → for a self-pay balance, Open → for a co-pay-only client.

Outstanding co-pays

/billing/copays

Every insured visit where a co-pay was due but hasn't come in. Record it when it arrives.

Controls

  • Received on date sets the day a collected co-pay books to (defaults today).
  • Search by client; toggle My clients / Everyone.

Actions

  • Collect records the co-pay as received. This is how you mark a co-pay paid.
  • Invoice bills it instead.
  • Tick several for the same client, then Create one invoice.

Clients

records

The roster is the master list; each client record holds everything a claim or invoice needs.

Client roster

/billing/clients

Every client in the practice. Open one, or tick several and build their claims in one run.

Columns

  • Client, Date of birth (+ age), Usual insurer (or Self-pay), Seen by, Paid (cash collected), Last visit.
  • Tap any heading to sort; a checkbox is disabled when a client has no insured sessions to claim.

Actions

  • Search, filter by insurer or clinician.
  • + New client adds a client to a clinician's book.
  • Tick several then Generate CMS-1500 to batch their claims.

Client record

/billing/clients/[id]

One client's full file. Sections top to bottom:

Referral banner
Always visible, payment-critical. Shows days left / expiring / expired. Visits after the end date can't be billed. Add / Renew referral.
Client record
Identity + insurance: DOB, sex, contact, address, insurer, member ID, relationship to insured. Edit details to change; a referral sub-block sets the billing window.
Insurance deductible
The insurer's annual figure counting down as the client pays out of pocket. Not money you hold. biller / owner / admin edit
Diagnoses
ICD-10 codes for box 21 of the claim. Anyone on the record can edit; changes are logged.
Documents
Intake form (auto-linked), referral letter, anything else. Upload a file (up to 4 MB) or add a link.
Team notes
Admin / billing notes the whole team sees (benefits, authorisations, insurer calls). Not for clinical or sensitive info.
Appointments & charges
Every date of service with its status pill. Tick insured visits for a claim, self-pay for an invoice. Per row: ⚠ after referral and ⚑ Read note flags, plus an inline Invoice link when that visit is owed. Change selected… bulk-edits ticked rows.
Emails sent
Every invoice the system emailed this client, with sent / failed status.
Session notes
Clinical, encrypted, treating clinicians only. A pure biller never sees these.
The status pill on each charge is the single source of truth for where that visit sits: Self-pay To bill Awaiting payment Collected Write-off.

Your month: commission earned, what's still owed to you, and where your cut came from.

On the page

  • Who owes you: insurers with outstanding claims, oldest first, and your cut on each.

Tickets

/team/tickets

The tracked, accountable channel for anything needing follow-through (payouts, HR, IT, billing issues).

Status lifecycle

  • Not started → Start
  • Being sorted → Mark done / Need info
  • Needs info / On hold → Resume
  • Done → Reopen

How it works

  • The "ball" sits with everyone who hasn't replied since the last comment. Yours shows Your turn; otherwise Waiting on… with a Nudge.
  • Edit your own posts within 10 minutes (or until someone replies).
  • The person who raised it can Mark done too. A done ticket is closed to comments until reopened.

Setup & worklist

configuration

The rules behind every claim, invoice and payout, and the shared list of what to build next.

Setup

/billing/configbiller / owner

What you see is scoped: as biller you set the claims, fees and practice details, not the owner's money-management sections.

Your sections

  • My rates: your commission % per clinician.
  • Practice details: name, address, contact, and the claim identifiers (Billing NPI box 33a, Tax ID box 25, taxonomy, rendering NPI per clinician box 24J). These print on claims and invoices.
  • Insurers & co-pay: each insurer's co-pay rule and payer claim code.
  • Service codes: CPT codes with time / fee options (first is the default).

Owner-only sections

  • Biller commission %, running expenses, and per-clinician splits (retention, pension, biller base).
  • You won't see these; they feed the payout math behind the scenes.
Every number traces back here. If a payout or claim looks wrong, the cause is almost always a rate or identifier on this page.

Money words

The terms behind the figures. All amounts are KYD.

Collected
Cash actually received: co-pays taken at the visit + insurance payments in. Only collected money pays anyone.
Insurance portion
The fee minus the contracted co-pay. An uncollected co-pay is the practice's loss, not extra insurance revenue.
Outstanding
Insurance not yet collected, either to bill or awaiting payment.
Retention
The company's cut of collected money (set per clinician). What's left after it is the clinician's share.
Write-off vs write-down
A claim settled for less than billed. Both are a separate bucket, reported on their own, never counted as collected and never paid out.
Your commission
Your cut per claim: each clinician's own rate on their insurance, plus a share of the company's retention where it applies. Charged on the after-retention share, not the gross.
Net payout
Commission earned minus the earner's own pension.
Aging buckets
How old an unpaid claim is by date of service: 0-14, 15-30, 31-60, and 60+ days (the one that matters).
